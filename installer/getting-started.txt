Home Network Monitor is ready
=============================

Open it from the Start menu (or the desktop shortcut if you chose one).
It opens in its own window and shows the devices on your home network.

First steps
-----------
1. Click "Scan now" to see what's connected. Auto-scan then checks again
   every minute while the window is open.
2. If the app finds a router it doesn't know yet, click "Add this router"
   and enter the router's login (usually printed on a sticker under it).
3. Use the tabs under each router for data balance, messages and settings.

Turning on the Ask AI tab (optional)
------------------------------------
Ask AI comes switched off: no AI service or key is included. To turn it
on, open the Ask AI tab under any router, then choose a service (NVIDIA,
OpenAI or Anthropic), type the model to use and paste your own API key.
Get a key from:

    NVIDIA     build.nvidia.com
    OpenAI     platform.openai.com/api-keys
    Anthropic  console.anthropic.com

Change any of these later with the "AI settings" button in the chat.

Your privacy
------------
Everything runs on this PC. Router logins, history and settings are stored
in %APPDATA%\Home Network Monitor and are kept if you uninstall, so a
reinstall picks up where you left off.

Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher the first time.
Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
